Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

What information I collect

When you create an account on Sbananas1, I collect your email address.

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. I do not have access to or store your password myself.

If you choose to receive website updates by email, I may also record that you have given permission to receive these emails and when you gave that permission.

Why I collect this information

Your email address is used to create and manage your account and allow you to sign in.

If you separately choose to receive website updates, your email address may also be used to send you occasional emails about new features and updates to Sbananas1.

You do not have to agree to receive these emails to create an account.

Who handles your information

Sbananas1 uses Google Firebase to provide account authentication and related website services. This means some personal information is processed by Google as part of providing these services.

If another service is used to send emails, this privacy policy will be updated to explain which service is used and what information it receives.

How long I keep your information

Account information is kept while your account remains active. Information that is no longer required will be deleted when reasonably possible, subject to any information that needs to be retained for legal or security purposes.

Emails and marketing

Optional website update or marketing emails will only be sent where you have chosen to receive them.

You can withdraw your permission at any time. Marketing emails will provide a way to stop receiving future marketing emails.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights relating to your personal information. These can include rights to access, correct or delete your information and to object to or restrict certain uses of it.

You can also withdraw your permission to receive marketing emails at any time.

Contact

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the website or the services it uses change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
